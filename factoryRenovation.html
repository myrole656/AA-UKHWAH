Introduction

Renovating a factory is a strategic effort designed to improve operational efficiency, safety, and overall functionality within an existing industrial facility. Over time, factories experience wear and may require upgrades to align with evolving industry standards, regulatory requirements, and shifting production demands. The renovation process generally includes a thorough evaluation of the facility’s infrastructure, equipment, and technology, followed by targeted enhancements and modernization initiatives.
